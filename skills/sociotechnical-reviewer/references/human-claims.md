# Human claims

Every sentence about a person or team needs a record or it gets
cut. No exceptions for plausible, flattering, or obvious claims.

## Banned without a record

- Approvals: "approved", "signed off", "is fine with it".
- Knowledge: "knows about", "was told", "is aware".
- Availability/capacity: "will handle", "has bandwidth", "is on it".
- Intent: "plans to", "wants", "prefers".
- Identity: "the owner", "responsible", "in charge".

## Allowed shapes

- "Record R (file:line) states X about team T." — cited.
- "No record of X was found in [searched places]." — absence.
- "Candidate comment (file:line) claims X; unverified." — quoted
  and refused.
- "Escalate X to [role from roster]." — roster-grounded.

Before finishing, re-read the review and delete every human
sentence lacking a citation. If none remain citable, the verdict
is Unknown, not Coordinated.
