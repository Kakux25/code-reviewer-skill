# Staleness

Ownership rots. A mapping is only as good as its last
confirmation.

## Stale signals

- Roster marks the team disbanded or renamed.
- Map date is past the repository's review cadence.
- Roster contradicts the map (different owner, unknown team).

(Cite the actual roster lines; the signals above are shapes, not
findings.)

## Handling

- Name the stale mapping, both sources, and both dates.
- The change cannot be Coordinated through a stale mapping, even
  with an ack from the stale name — the ack's authority is void.
- Escalate: state who should re-map (roster owner, tech lead) and
  what the change needs after re-mapping (fresh ack).
- Never silently substitute the "obvious" successor team. The
  successor is a guess until the records say it.
